Our Philosophy

Redefining Dental Care

We believe dentistry should be a calming, transformative experience that supports your overall health and wellbeing.

Our Story

Founded in 2010 by Dr. Julian Aura, Aura Dental began as a vision to transform the dental experience from something to endure into something to embrace.

After years of witnessing patients' dental anxiety and recognizing how traditional dental practices often exacerbated fear, Dr. Aura set out to create a different kind of dental practice—one that prioritizes the nervous system as much as the teeth.

2010

Aura Dental founded with one treatment room

2018

Expanded to current 8,000 sq ft facility

The Aura Dental Difference

Our approach is built on three foundational principles that guide every aspect of your care.

Neuroscience-Based Care

We design every aspect of your visit to keep your nervous system at ease, from ambient lighting to noise management and gentle techniques.

Biological Dentistry

We use biocompatible materials and consider the mouth-body connection for treatments that support overall health rather than just fixing symptoms.

Patient-Centered Approach

We take time to listen, explain, and ensure you feel heard and comfortable throughout your dental journey. Your comfort is our priority.

A Healing Environment

Our clinic is designed as a healing environment, not a clinical space. From ergonomic chairs with memory foam to ambient lighting that mimics natural daylight, every detail is considered for your comfort.

  • HEPA air filtration with UV-C sanitization
  • Noise-canceling headphones and silent dental tools
  • Warm blankets and comfort items in every room
  • Visible sterilization theater for complete transparency
